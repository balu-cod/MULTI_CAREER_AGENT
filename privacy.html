Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 | Last Updated: November 13, 2026

1. Overview

The Multi-Agent Career System ("we", "our", or "the Platform") is an AI-powered autonomous agent system designed to help candidates discover relevant job postings, customize resumes for ATS compliance, dispatch job applications directly from their verified email address, track interview responses from recruiters, and receive personalized career upskilling recommendations.

2. Information We Access and Process

  • Google Identity & Profile Information: When you authenticate via Google OAuth 2.0, we receive your email address, full name, and avatar URL to identify your candidate session.
  • Gmail API & Email Credentials: If you connect via Google OAuth (with Gmail scopes) or provide a 16-character Google App Password, our agents access your email solely on your behalf to:
    • Dispatch tailored application emails and resumes directly from your own Gmail address to hiring managers.
    • Scan incoming recruiter responses (interview invitations, assessments, rejection notices, offers) to update your live Application Tracker vault.
  • Candidate Profile & Resume Data: Skills, education, work history, and target roles that you upload or configure to match against job descriptions.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

The Platform's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only access Gmail messages necessary to detect recruiter responses related to job applications.
  • We never sell, rent, or lease your personal data or email contents to third parties or advertising networks.
  • We do not use your email data to train generalized AI foundation models without explicit permission.
  • Session tokens and App Passwords are encrypted and isolated per user session.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your credentials and application records are stored with local database isolation and TLS encryption. You can disconnect your Gmail account or revoke access at any time through your Google Account Security settings or directly inside the platform Settings.
